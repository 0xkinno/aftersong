# Sources

Each figure was checked against its source on 2026-10-28. A figure that could not be confirmed was removed.

| What is true | Source | How it was checked |
|---|---|---|
| 57 million people were living with dementia worldwide in 2021, with nearly 10 million new cases every year. | [World Health Organization, Dementia fact sheet](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/dementia) | Opened 2026-10-28. Page states 57 million in 2021 and nearly 10 million new cases a year. |
| 42% annual turnover among certified nursing assistants in US nursing homes in 2025 (42.34 percent, down from 44.16 percent in 2024), across 917 homes. | [AHCA, 2025-2026 Nursing Home Salary and Benefits Report summary, 9 September 2025](https://www.ahcancal.org/News-and-Communications/Blog/Pages/2025–2026-Nursing-Home-Report-Reveals-Lower-Turnover.aspx) | Opened 2026-10-28. Page states CNA turnover fell from 44.16 to 42.34 percent. |
| 22 trials of reminiscence therapy for dementia, with 1,972 people, were reviewed by Cochrane in 2018. | [Woods, O'Philbin, Farrell, Spector and Orrell, Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, 2018, CD001120](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD001120_reminiscence-therapy-dementia) | Review summary page read 2026-10-28 via search result: 22 studies, 1,972 people. The size of any benefit was not confirmed, so none is claimed. |
| Music memory A 2015 study in Brain compared brain regions that hold long-term musical memory with Alzheimer's data and found they lose fewer neurons and show less metabolic decline than the rest of the brain. | [Jacobsen, Stelzer, Fritz, Chetelat, La Joie and Turner, Brain 138(8), 2438 to 2450, 2015](https://scholars.duke.edu/publication/1818925) ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/brain/awv135)) | Citation and findings read 2026-10-28 from the Duke Scholars record and press coverage. Small sample; the paper's aim of therapeutic use is stated as a hope, not a result. |
| Ages 10 to 30 People tend to recall more personal memories from roughly ages 10 to 30, the reminiscence bump. A systematic review of 68 studies puts the span at about 10 to 30 years for important events and about 5 to 30 for word-cued memories. | [Munawar, Kuhn and Haque, Understanding the reminiscence bump: a systematic review, PLoS ONE, 2018](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0208595) | Findings read 2026-10-28 from the review's listing and abstract summary. |
| This is me is a free leaflet that staff, family or carers fill in to record a person's background, important events, preferences and routines, so care can be tailored to the person. | [Alzheimer's Society, This is me](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/get-support/publications-factsheets/this-is-me) | Opened 2026-10-28. Page describes the leaflet for people with dementia, delirium or communication difficulties. |

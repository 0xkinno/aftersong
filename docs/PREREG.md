# PREREG.md

Pre-registration for the findings in `FINDINGS.md`. This file is committed **before** the first live run
of `scripts/probe-matrix.mjs`. The script refuses to run unless this file is committed and unchanged,
and `FINDINGS.md` cites the commit hash it found. Questions, methods and decision rules below are
fixed. Negative and null results are reported with the same prominence as positive ones.

## Inputs
- The 15 curated intake forms in `data/eval/forms.json` (hash in `data/eval/PREREG.sha256.json`, committed first).
- Each form's favourites are resolved with `/search`; only exact single name matches are used. A form with no usable favourite is skipped and counted.
- The window for each form is the years the person was 10 to 30.
- Qloo calls follow the rules in `backend/src/qloo/guard.ts`: 250 ms spacing, no retry on 429, batched lookups.
- Signals are routed by domain: music and film favourites drive music, film and TV; food favourites drive only food and places.

## Definitions
- **Top eight**: the eight highest-ranked items in a query, ranked by `query.affinity` read from that call only.
- **Widely known**: `popularity` at or above 0.85.
- **His own signals**: the form's confirmed media favourites. **Cohort only**: `signal.demographics.age=55_and_older` and nothing else.
- **Same window**: identical `filter.release_year.min/max` where the type honours it.
- **Statistics**: exact two-sided binomial sign test (ties dropped) and Kendall tau, standard library only (`backend/src/stats.ts`). Significance threshold 0.05, no correction, because each question is tested once on its own.

## Questions
| ID | Question | Method | Reported | What would count against us |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | Does the age-cohort signal alone pull an older person's card toward famous defaults? | For each form, film and music: popularity of the top eight under cohort only versus under his own signals, same window. Paired by form. Sign test on (cohort minus own) mean popularity | Mean popularity both ways, mean count of widely known items in the top eight both ways, n, positives, negatives, ties, p | p at or above 0.05, or more forms with own signals more popular than cohort only |
| F2 | How many of the items Aftersong places would a generic oldies list ever contain? | Generic list proxy: the union of every item returned by any cohort-only query across all forms (film, TV, music). For each card, the share of its dated items that are in the union. Cards are built with no model | Mean overlap per card, union size, items in union, items total. Lower is more personal | A high mean overlap, meaning cards mostly contain what a cohort-only query also returns |
| F3 | Does one remembered favourite in music move film and TV choices inside the window? | For forms with at least one music and one film favourite: one film pool, pinned with `filter.results.entities`, ranked twice, with film anchors only and with the music anchor added. Kendall tau between the two orderings | Mean tau, forms where the order changed, forms skipped | Mean tau near 1 and few forms changed, meaning the music anchor does not move film |
| F4 | Does the year filter hold at the edges? | For film and TV, request the form's window and count returned items with a release year outside it. Items with no year are not counted as violations and are reported separately | Violations over dated items, per type | Any violation. One is enough to report that the filter does not hold |
| F5 | Does a hometown signal on an open query return dishes and places from that region rather than the care home's city? | For forms with a resolvable hometown locality and care locality: an open place query with `signal.location` set to the hometown locality and the hometown cuisine tags as a filter, never pinned. Share of returned places whose address text contains a hometown region token, and share containing the care city token | Both shares, places with no address | A hometown share not above the care city share |

## Rules for the report
1. `FINDINGS.md` is generated by the script from `docs/matrix-summary.json`. No number is typed.
2. The landing page discovery sentence is written only if F1 is significant in the predicted direction. Otherwise the landing page keeps its plain claim, and FINDINGS says why.
3. Anything that fails or is skipped is listed, with counts.
4. If a Qloo parameter is silently ignored, that is reported as a finding, not hidden.
5. Re-running with new forms makes a new pre-registration and a new commit. This one is not edited after the first run.
